Before you start

  • Each student needs a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge, and an internet connection. Phones and tablets cannot share a screen, so they cannot sit the exam.
  • The staff passcode is asked for every time an exam is started, so a student cannot start one from a shared lab PC.

Running the exam

  1. 1

    Create the exam

    Click Start an online exam and enter the passcode. Give the exam a name, set the minutes and type the questions. Leave a blank line between questions and put a * before the right answer.

    What is the worst-case time complexity of quicksort?
- O(n)
- O(n log n)
* O(n^2)
- O(log n)
  2. 2

    Read out the code

    You get a six-character code and your dashboard. Keep the dashboard open on your screen for the whole exam.

  3. 3

    Students join

    Students go to {{ site }}, open Exam mode and type the code, then their name. They click Share screen & begin and choose Entire Screen. Each student's time starts at that moment.

  4. 4

    Watch the room

    Each student appears as a tile that shows clear, warning or flagged. Use Allow lookup for an open-book part, or Pause to stop checking. Click a tile to do this for one student.

  5. 5

    See the results

    When students submit, they see their score and a timeline of what was recorded. Your dashboard shows who has submitted and how many flags each student has. Click End exam & download CSV to finish early or close the completed exam. The results CSV downloads automatically. After checking it, confirm the download to permanently delete that exam's records from the app.

What students are told They must share their entire screen; a single tab or window is refused. The screen is checked every few seconds for other windows. No image or video is sent or saved, only when another window was on top and for how long. A warning comes first, and closing the window in time keeps it off the record.
